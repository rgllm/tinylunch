TinyLunch is an English-language iPhone app. It estimates calories from a meal photo with Apple Intelligence on the device.

Contact

Photo estimates

Take a photo or choose one image from your library. Camera access is requested only when you tap Take photo. The photo picker is limited to the image you choose.

New estimates need iOS 27 and Apple Intelligence with on-device vision.

  • If Apple Intelligence is off, turn it on in Settings, then retry.
  • If Apple’s on-device model is not ready, allow its system download to finish, then retry. Apple manages that download.
  • If this iPhone cannot run the on-device model, or the current language is unsupported, saved meals remain available and new estimates wait.
  • If a photo cannot be estimated, it stays in the draft so you can retry. A photo that cannot be read, or a request the model declines, asks you to try another photo.

The app suggests foods, portions in grams, and preparation assumptions. You can correct a name, change the grams, remove a false detection, or add a missed item by name. Changing only the grams recalculates on the iPhone. Changing the food or preparation asks the on-device model for a new estimate. If that estimate cannot run, the previous draft stays unchanged.

An item without a usable estimate stays unknown. It is not counted as zero calories. The screen labels that result Incomplete estimate and shows a known-foods subtotal. If nothing can be estimated, there is no numeric total.

Calories are model estimates, not laboratory measurements or medical advice. TinyLunch is not a medical device.

History

Nothing is written to History until you tap Save. A saved meal keeps foods, portions, preparation notes, and calorie estimates. It does not keep the photo.

History stays on this iPhone, in protected storage, and is excluded from device backups. Saved meals can be viewed. They are not edited after saving. Delete a meal from History and confirm the deletion; that removes it from the archive. Deleting TinyLunch removes the archive. History still opens when photo analysis is unavailable.

Choosing an iCloud-only library photo may need Apple’s system to download that file before import. That download is iOS, not TinyLunch. If it cannot be imported, choose another photo or use the camera.

Privacy

The privacy policy describes what stays on the device. The privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data types (Data Not Collected).
