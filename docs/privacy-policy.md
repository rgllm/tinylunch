# TinyLunch Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 September 2026

TinyLunch is a local iPhone app published by Rogério Moreira (me@rgllm.com). It estimates calories from a meal photo using Apple Intelligence on the device.

## Data TinyLunch does not collect

TinyLunch does not create an account and does not send your photos, meal history, or analytics to a server operated by us. There is no advertising, tracking, crash reporter, or cloud backup of your meals.

## Photos

If you take a photo or choose one from your library, TinyLunch keeps that image in memory long enough to estimate the meal or until you discard it. The app does not save photos into History and does not upload them.

Choosing an iCloud-only library photo may require Apple’s system to download that file. That transfer is handled by iOS, not by TinyLunch’s own network code.

## Saved meals

History is stored only on your iPhone, in the app’s protected storage, and only after you tap Save. A saved meal includes foods, portions, preparation notes, and calorie estimates. It does not include the photo.

History is excluded from device backups. Deleting a meal removes it from the archive. Deleting TinyLunch removes the archive.

## On-device estimates

Photo analysis and text corrections use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models / Apple Intelligence. Apple may need to download its model assets the first time. Those downloads are managed by Apple. TinyLunch does not use Private Cloud Compute or a third-party nutrition API.

Calorie figures are estimates, not laboratory measurements or medical advice. TinyLunch is not a medical device.

## Permissions

- **Camera** is requested only when you tap Take photo, so TinyLunch can photograph a meal.
- **Photo library** access uses the system picker for the image you choose. TinyLunch does not ask for your full library.

TinyLunch does not request microphone, location, tracking, or HealthKit access.
