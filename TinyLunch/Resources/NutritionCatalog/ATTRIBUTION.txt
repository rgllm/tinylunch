This is a derived identity-and-energy extract, not the full USDA datasets.
The version directory includes the source archive URLs, SHA-256 checksums,
record counts, and the checksum of catalog.json in manifest.json.

Energy is kcal per 100 grams of edible food. SR Legacy uses nutrient 1008.
Foundation Foods prefers 2048 (Atwater specific factors), then 2047
(Atwater general factors), then 1008 when that is the only available value.
Energy measures are never added together. Records without a valid energy
value are excluded, not assigned zero. A true source zero is retained.
Raw and cooked foods remain separate records. Source description words
supply preparation labels. A missing preparation label is not inferred.

TinyLunch uses this resource entirely offline. A food match and a portion
remain estimates even when energy density comes from USDA. Many Portuguese
mixed dishes are absent. INSA PortFIR is not included.
